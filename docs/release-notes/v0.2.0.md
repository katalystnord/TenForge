TenForge 0.2.0: compression and bending join tension, for files from any
testing machine.

- New procedures: ISO 604:2002 (plastics) and ISO 844:2021 (rigid foams)
  in compression; ISO 178:2019 (plastics, three-point) and ISO 14125:1998
  (fibre composites, three- and four-point) in bending.
- The window asks for the procedure first, then shows only what that
  test needs: a bend test asks for the support span, the loading span and
  where the deflection was measured (at the loading points, as a
  crosshead records it, or at mid-span); a compression test for the
  length the deformation is measured over.
- Compression and bending recorded as negative are analysed positive,
  and the notes say so; the record keeps the file's signs.
- Machine compliance: a table of the machine's own deflection against
  force is taken out of the travel. It is never extrapolated, and a
  record keeps it.
- Foam strains are counted from where the steepest straight part meets
  zero force (ISO 844); ISO 178's stress at conventional deflection is
  taken at 1.5 times the thickness for each specimen.
- Fixture checks (ISO 178 span to thickness, ISO 14125 loading span a
  third of the span) are reported, and a failed one is a reason the
  results are not to the standard.
- ZwickRoell testXpert exports read directly, with the specimen's size
  from their header; column names in German and French are recognised.
- Figures for analysis and reports: the chart shades the stretches the
  analysis identified (the modulus fit, the toe, uniform elongation and
  necking, after the break) and labels each strength. Drag the modulus
  window's edges on the chart, or type it, and the modulus refits; the
  results and the record say the window was yours. Add your own notes at
  a strain or over a span. File > Export chart writes SVG or PDF as
  vector figures, or PNG; the report's chart is vector too.
- `tf-analyse` gains --width, --thickness, --span, --loading-span,
  --deflection-at and --compliance, and runs the window's own analysis.

Validated against ZwickRoell testXpert III's own evaluation of TU
Braunschweig's four-point bending tests: 23 specimens, 161 comparisons
(Ef, the stresses at both ends of the modulus window, R2, flexural
strength and strain, and the number of samples in each modulus fit),
none outside half a unit of the last digit testXpert printed. Compression
has no reference evaluation yet; see docs/validation/phase-3.md for what
is and is not validated.

**Every result is still "aligned with" its standard, not "to" it**: the
procedures' parameters come from public sources and stay unconfirmed until
checked against the purchased standards.

The AppImage is for x86_64 Linux, built on Ubuntu 26.04, as for 0.1.
Files only, one test at a time; running a machine comes in 0.4. The
manual is in docs/manual/.
